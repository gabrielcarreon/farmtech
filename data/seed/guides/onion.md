## Climate Requirements
Bulb onion in this sample needs a dry finish. Rainfall requirements are 300 to 500 millimetres, and temperature requirements are 15 to 27 degrees Celsius. Nueva Ecija plantings cluster in October, November, and December. Prolonged rain during bulbing causes splits and storage loss. Laguna is marked unsuitable in the sample regional table because the wetter climate fights that dry-down. A weather score can still be computed, and the result should say the regional flag lowered suitability.

## Water Requirements
Irrigate lightly and stop in time for the necks to fall. Waterlogging is more damaging than a slightly dry week late in the crop. Do not read a single dry planting day as a full-season result. The growing period for the sample red onion is about 110 days. Days beyond the forecast must use climate normals and lower data confidence when they dominate the calendar.

## Planting
Onion is capital intensive in the sample cost table, especially seed and labor. Compare estimated cost with available capital and flag a gap instead of silently shrinking the budget. Raised beds help drainage. This text does not set a contract price. Stored prices swing widely in the sample series, so market risk should stay visible even when a news note is calm.

## Harvest
Lift bulbs after the tops fall and cure them before bagging. Wet curing invites rot that never appears in the planting-week forecast. The sample yield band is about 8000 to 15000 kilograms per hectare in stronger provinces and weaker where the regional record is poorer. Store the source of the yield rows with the analysis so a later review can see they were sample data.
