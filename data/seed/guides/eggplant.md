## Climate Requirements
Eggplant in this sample grows at 20 to 32 degrees Celsius. Rainfall requirements are 450 to 800 millimetres across the crop. Planting months stored for the crop are January, February, October, November, and December. The crop can set fruit over a long period, but the sample variety is scored on about 100 days so the scenario stays bounded. Temperature inside the band plus a few extreme days should both appear in the weather section.

## Water Requirements
Flowering plants drop blossoms if the soil dries out completely, and they also suffer when beds stay saturated. Mulch can steady moisture. Do not equate a rainy planting day with a rainy harvest month. Forecast days and climate-normal days are labeled separately on the timeline for that reason. Pest pressure rises when foliage stays wet, which is a production-risk discussion, not a reason to invent a yield loss number that the tables do not contain.

## Planting
Transplant after the seedlings have a firm stem. The sample yield band is wide, about 15000 to 28000 kilograms per hectare, so production risk stays material even in a friendly season. Costs in the sample are mid-range compared with onion. Fruit is picked often, so labor is a real share of cost and the cost row should be read as a package, not only seed.

## Harvest
Pick fruit before seed hardens, and do not leave over-mature fruit on the plant. A week of missed harvest lowers quality even when the weather score is high. Market sentiment can be positive while the price range is still wide. Show both. This guide is a sample text for retrieval, not a substitute for the structured price and yield tables.
