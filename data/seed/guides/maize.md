## Climate Requirements
The sample maize crop prefers 18 to 32 degrees Celsius. Rainfall requirements over the crop are 500 to 800 millimetres, with the largest need around tasseling. Suitable planting months in the dataset include November, December, January, May, and June. A hot extreme, defined in the active scoring profile, should count against weather suitability even if the average day sits inside the band. Central Luzon climate normals are drier in the first quarter than in July and August.

## Water Requirements
Moisture stress at silking cuts kernel set. Water that stands for days on a heavy soil is a different problem and can stunt roots. The sample variety needs about 105 days. If a farm horizon clips that duration, say so in the assumptions. Irrigation cost in the cost table is one component of the per-hectare total and should not be double-counted in profit.

## Planting
Plant into moisture, not into a dust layer or a puddled furrow. Keep the population even so the yield band remains interpretable. This note does not publish a selling price. Use the maize rows in the market dataset, which cluster in a narrower band than onion. That narrower dispersion is why market risk can be lower even when sentiment is only neutral.

## Harvest
Shell at a safe moisture and dry grain before storage. Delayed harvest in a wet week invites ear rot. Sample yields are about 4000 to 7000 kilograms per hectare. Report the range. A single expected kilogram figure hides the spread the risk engine is using.
